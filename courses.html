How the challenges work

1. Study the lesson

Read the rules and examples, then write your own answers to the practice questions. Their answers unlock only after your first challenge attempt.

2. Take the challenge

One question at a time, shuffled every attempt. Typed answers must be exact. Wrong first time? You get one more try for half a point.

3. Learn from the feedback

Every mistake shows what not to do and the rule to follow, including the traps Arabic speakers fall into most.

4. Pass, then move on

80% or more passes the module. Pass all three to unlock your level certificate. We warn you if you skip ahead.
