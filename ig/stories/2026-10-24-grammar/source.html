CORRECT
Hardly had I arrived when the phone rang.
THE RULE
A negative adverb at the start (Hardly, Rarely, Never) triggers inversion: auxiliary + subject.
Hardly … whenNo sooner … than
SAME RULE
Never have I seen such a mess.
Rarely does she complain.
Not only did he apologise, he paid.
