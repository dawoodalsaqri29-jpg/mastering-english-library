Corrected text
Yesterday I went to the market and bought many things for my family.
What I changed
go → went  past tense; “yesterday” needs past
buyed → bought  irregular verb
thing → things  plural; you said “many”
استخدمت present tense بدل past tense، وكتبت صيغة خاطئة للفعل “buy”
