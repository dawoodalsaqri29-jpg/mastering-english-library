# Client hunt, 2026-11-07

Targets: English institutes, tutoring centres and schools in the Gulf, and Arabic-speaking learners (B1-C1).
Lap 1 found nothing because the session network blocked page fetches; the network was opened and the scouts
ran a second lap. 17 leads found, 4 survived the Teardown, 12 secret ballots.

| Lead | Points | Said yes | Budget | Fit | Timing |
|------|--------|----------|--------|-----|--------|
| IFI IDP Qatar (Doha) | 35 | 11 of 12 | 8 | 8 | 8 |

The 12 never all agreed on any three. Only IFI IDP Qatar has a strong majority. The two runners-up rest on
teacher job ads, which most voters did not accept as a sign they will buy materials. No replacement lead was
left to bring in.

## 1. IFI IDP Qatar: https://ifi.qa/
- Why they will pay: they launched new IELTS and English courses in Doha this autumn, charge QAR 600-4,800
  a course, and their course page names no coursebook.
- Proof: Zawya press release, 2026-10-01 (https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/ifi-idp-qatar-expands-english-language-and-ielts-preparation-services-in-doha-1460646);
  course page published 2026-09-22, updated 2026-10-21 (https://ifi.qa/english-ielts-classes-qatar/).
- Doubt: IDP gives away free IELTS prep material; groups are small (up to 4).
- Contact: info@ifi.qa, +974 4037 9708, Floor 16, Laffan Tower, West Bay.

## 2. Britain International Academy: https://www.bia.edu.kw/

## 3. Mind Base Education: https://mindbase.education/
- Why they might pay: hiring an English teacher who will "select appropriate teaching materials" for IELTS,

## What the Teardown threw out most
Freelance briefs that want new custom content (or are frozen), signals older than 60 days, and chains such
as Edoxi. Trustpilot, Reddit, Bayt, LinkedIn and Indeed block automated access, so reviews and most job
boards could not be checked.
