C2Writing

Style, register and concision

Edit for precision: cut wordiness, control register and vary sentence rhythm.

Download PDF

At C2 level, good writing is about control. Every word should earn its place, and the register should match the reader exactly.

Cut wordiness

WordyConcise
due to the fact thatbecause
in the event thatif
at this point in timenow
make a decisiondecide
it is important to note that(delete it)

Control register

The same idea changes form depending on the reader.

RegisterExample
CasualThe plan fell apart pretty fast.
NeutralThe plan failed quickly.
FormalThe initiative proved unsustainable within a short period.

Vary rhythm

Long sentences build an argument. Short ones land it. Alternating the two keeps a reader alert.

Avoid elegant variation. Do not change a key term just to avoid repetition. If you call it the policy, keep calling it the policy.

Practice

  1. Make concise: Due to the fact that it was raining, the match was cancelled.
  2. Make concise: We need to make a decision at this point in time.
  3. Rewrite formally: Sales went through the roof.
Show answers
  1. Because it was raining, the match was cancelled. / The match was rained off.
  2. We need to decide now.
  3. Sales increased dramatically.
