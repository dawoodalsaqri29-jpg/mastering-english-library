Travel and airport vocabulary

The words and phrases you need from check-in to arrival, and the verbs that go with them.

Download PDF

Travel English is very predictable: the same words appear on signs, screens and announcements in every airport. Learn them in the order of your journey.

StageKey wordsExample
Bookingone-way, return, aisle seat, window seatI'd like a return ticket with an aisle seat, please.
Check-incheck in, luggage, hand luggage, boarding passYou can check in online and print your boarding pass.
Securitygo through security, liquids, scannerPlease put liquids in a clear bag.
Departuregate, board, delayed, on timeFlight EK12 is now boarding at gate 22.
On the planetake off, land, seat belt, cabin crewPlease fasten your seat belt; we are about to take off.
Arrivalpassport control, baggage claim, customsAfter passport control, collect your bags from baggage claim.
Learn the verb with the noun. You catch or miss a flight, board a plane, check in your bags and collect them on arrival.
Common mistakes. Luggage and baggage are uncountable: say two pieces of luggage or two bags, not two luggages. And a plane takes off; it does not fly up.

Practice

  1. Where do you collect your suitcase after the flight?
  2. Complete: Hurry up or we'll ___ the flight!
  3. Correct the mistake: I have three luggages.
  4. What is the opposite of "take off" for a plane?
  5. Ask for a seat next to the window at check-in.
Show answers
  1. At baggage claim.
  2. miss
  3. I have three bags. / I have three pieces of luggage.
  4. land
  5. Could I have a window seat, please?
