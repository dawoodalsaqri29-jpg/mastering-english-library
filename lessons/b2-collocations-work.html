Collocations for work and study

Natural word partnerships such as 'meet a deadline' and 'raise an issue'.

Collocations are words that naturally go together. Using them makes your English sound fluent rather than translated.

  • meet a deadlinephrase

    to finish work by the agreed time

    We worked late to meet the deadline.

  • raise an issuephrase

    to mention a problem for discussion

    She raised an issue about the budget.

  • make progressphrase

    to improve or move forward

    You've made real progress this term.

  • take responsibilityphrase

    to accept that something is your duty or fault

    The manager took responsibility for the error.

  • do researchphrase

    to study a subject carefully to find facts

    I'm doing research on renewable energy.

  • reach an agreementphrase

    to agree after discussion

    The two sides finally reached an agreement.

  • gain experiencephrase

    to get knowledge or skill by doing something

    An internship helps you gain experience.

  • pay attentionphrase

    to watch or listen carefully

    Pay attention to the instructions.

  • set a goalphrase

    to decide on something you want to achieve

    Set a realistic goal for each week.

  • hold a meetingphrase

    to organise and have a meeting

    We hold a team meeting every Monday.

Practice

  1. We need to ___ a decision by Friday. (make / do)
  2. She ___ a lot of experience abroad. (gained / won)
  3. Who will ___ responsibility for this project? (take / get)

  2. gained
