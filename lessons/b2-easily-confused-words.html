Easily confused words

Pairs that Arabic speakers often mix up, such as borrow and lend, rob and steal, and say and tell.

Download PDF

Some English words look or feel alike but are used differently. Many of these pairs share one word in Arabic, so they are easy to confuse.

  • borrow / lendverb

    borrow = take for a short time; lend = give for a short time

    Can I borrow your car? / Can you lend me your car?

  • rob / stealverb

    rob a person or place; steal a thing

    They robbed the bank and stole $10,000.

  • say / tellverb

    say something; tell someone something

    He said he was tired. He told me he was tired.

  • make / doverb

    make = create or produce; do = activities and work

    make a mistake, make a cake / do homework, do the shopping

  • bring / takeverb

    bring = towards the speaker; take = away from the speaker

    Bring it here. Take it to the office.

  • lose / missverb

    lose = no longer have; miss = not catch, or feel sad without

    I lost my keys. I missed the bus. I miss my family.

  • fun / funnyadjective

    fun = enjoyable; funny = makes you laugh

    The party was fun. The comedian was funny.

  • affect / effectverb / noun

    affect is usually the verb; effect is usually the noun

    Stress affects sleep. Stress has an effect on sleep.

  • economic / economicaladjective

    economic = about the economy; economical = saves money

    economic growth / an economical car

  • sensible / sensitiveadjective

    sensible = wise; sensitive = easily hurt, or aware of feelings

    a sensible decision / sensitive skin

Learn them in a sentence. Memorise a short example such as Can I borrow your pen? rather than a translation. The pattern sticks better than the word.

Practice

  1. Could you ___ me some money until Friday? (borrow / lend)
  2. Someone ___ my wallet on the train. (robbed / stole)
  3. She ___ me the meeting was cancelled. (said / told)
  4. I always ___ mistakes with prepositions. (make / do)
  5. Don't forget to ___ your umbrella when you go out. (bring / take)
  6. Buying in bulk is more ___ . (economic / economical)
Show answers
  1. lend
  2. stole
  3. told
  4. make
  5. take
  6. economical
