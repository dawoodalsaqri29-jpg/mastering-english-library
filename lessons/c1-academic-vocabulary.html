Academic vocabulary for essays

High-value words from academic English that lift the register of your writing.

These words appear frequently in academic texts across all subjects. Swap them in for simpler words to make your essays more precise.

  • substantialadj.

    large in amount or importance

    There is substantial evidence for this claim.

  • undermineverb

    to weaken gradually

    Constant criticism can undermine confidence.

  • inherentadj.

    existing as a natural, permanent part

    Every investment carries inherent risk.

  • facilitateverb

    to make a process easier

    Technology can facilitate learning.

  • discrepancynoun

    a difference between things that should match

    There was a discrepancy between the two reports.

  • advocateverb

    to publicly support an idea

    Many experts advocate earlier intervention.

  • plausibleadj.

    seeming reasonable or likely

    This is a plausible explanation.

  • mitigateverb

    to make something less harmful

    Measures were taken to mitigate the impact.

  • notionnoun

    an idea or belief

    The notion of free will is debated.

  • comprehensiveadj.

    including everything necessary

    The report gives a comprehensive overview.

  • subsequentlyadv.

    after that; later

    The data was subsequently analysed.

  • constrainverb

    to limit or restrict

    Our research was constrained by funding.

Practice

  1. Replace the simple word: The new road will make easier trade between the towns.
  2. Replace the simple word: Several studies support a four-day week.
  3. Replace the simple word: The results weaken the original theory.
