Articles: a, an, the or no article

When to use a, an, the or no article at all, with the slip Arabic speakers make most.

Download PDF

English has two articles: a/an (indefinite) and the (definite). Many nouns also take no article at all. The choice depends on whether the listener knows which thing you mean.

ArticleUseExample
a / anOne thing, mentioned for the first timeI saw a dog in the park.
a / anJobs and descriptionsShe's an engineer. It's a good idea.
theSomething already mentioned, or clear from the situationThe dog was huge. Can you close the door?
theOnly one exists; superlatives; ordinal numbersthe sun, the best day, the first time
no articlePlural and uncountable nouns used in generalDogs are loyal. Water is essential.
A or an? Choose by sound, not spelling: a university (you-), a one-way ticket (wun-), an hour (silent h), an MBA (em-).
Common mistake. Arabic uses al- for general ideas, so learners write The life is hard or I like the coffee. In English, general ideas take no article: Life is hard. I like coffee.

Practice

  1. I bought ___ new laptop last week. ___ laptop is very fast.
  2. She wants to be ___ architect.
  3. ___ honesty is important in ___ friendship.
  4. This is ___ best film I've ever seen.
  5. We stayed in ___ hotel near ___ Nile.
  6. Correct the mistake: The children need the love and the attention.
Show answers
  1. a, The
  2. an
  3. no article, no article (Honesty is important in friendship.)
  4. the
  5. a, the (rivers take the)
  6. Children need love and attention.
