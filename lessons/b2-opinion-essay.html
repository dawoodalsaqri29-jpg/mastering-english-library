Writing an opinion essay

A four-paragraph plan and linking words for clear, persuasive essays.

Download PDF

An opinion essay states a clear view and supports it with reasons and examples. Examiners look for organisation, linking and a consistent position.

Four-paragraph plan

ParagraphContent
1. IntroductionRephrase the question and state your opinion.
2. First reasonTopic sentence, explanation, example.
3. Second reasonTopic sentence, explanation, example. Optionally mention and answer an opposing view.
4. ConclusionRestate your opinion in new words and summarise the reasons.

Linking words

FunctionExpressions
Giving opinionIn my view, / I strongly believe that / It seems to me that
AddingFurthermore, / In addition, / What is more,
ContrastingHowever, / Nevertheless, / Although / On the other hand,
ExamplesFor instance, / such as / A good example of this is
ConcludingTo sum up, / All in all, / In conclusion,
Keep one position. You can mention the other side, but always explain why your view is stronger.

Practice

  1. Join with a contrast word: Cars are convenient. They pollute the air.
  2. Rewrite the opinion more formally: I think school uniforms are good.
  3. Which paragraph should contain your restated opinion and summary?
Show answers
  1. Cars are convenient. However, they pollute the air. / Although cars are convenient, they pollute the air.
  2. In my view, school uniforms are beneficial.
  3. The conclusion, paragraph 4.
