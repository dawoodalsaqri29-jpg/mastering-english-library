Linking words: and, but, because, so

Join short sentences into clear, connected writing with the most useful linking words and the right punctuation.

Download PDF

Writing that is only short sentences sounds like a list. Linking words show how your ideas connect: adding, contrasting, giving a reason or a result. A few well-chosen linkers make a big difference in emails and exam answers.

PurposeLinking wordsExample
Addingand, also, too, as wellThe hotel was clean. It was also very quiet.
Contrastbut, although, howeverAlthough it rained, we enjoyed the trip.
Reasonbecause, because of, asThe flight was late because of the fog.
Resultso, that's whyI was tired, so I went to bed early.
Orderfirst, then, after that, finallyFirst we had lunch. Then we went to the museum.
Punctuation. However starts a new sentence and takes a comma: The food was good. However, it was expensive. Although joins two parts of one sentence.
Common mistakes. Because of is followed by a noun, because by a clause: because of the traffic but because the traffic was bad. And don't use two linkers together: Although it was cold, but we swam is wrong; use only one.

Practice

  1. Join with "so": It was late. We took a taxi.
  2. Join with "although": The test was hard. I passed.
  3. Correct the mistake: I stayed home because of I was ill.
  4. Correct the mistake: Although the room was small, but it was comfortable.
  5. Rewrite with "However": The phone is cheap. The battery is poor.
Show answers
  1. It was late, so we took a taxi.
  2. Although the test was hard, I passed.
  3. I stayed home because I was ill. (or: because of illness)
  4. Although the room was small, it was comfortable.
  5. The phone is cheap. However, the battery is poor.
