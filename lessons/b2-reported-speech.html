Reported speech

Report statements, questions and requests, and know when the tense moves back.

Download PDF

We use reported speech to tell someone what another person said. With a past reporting verb such as said or asked, the verb in the reported part usually moves one step back in time.

Direct speechReported speech
"I'm tired."She said (that) she was tired.
"We'll call you."They said they would call me.
"I've finished."He said he had finished.
"I saw it yesterday."She said she had seen it the day before.
"I can help."He said he could help.

Questions

Yes/no questions take if or whether. Wh- questions keep the question word. Both use statement word order, with no do, does or did.

Direct questionReported question
"Are you ready?"He asked if I was ready.
"Where do you live?"She asked where I lived.

Requests and orders

Use ask or tell + person + to + verb. For negatives, put not before to.

DirectReported
"Please sit down."He asked me to sit down.
"Don't be late."She told us not to be late.

Time and place words

DirectReported
nowthen, at that time
today / tonightthat day / that night
tomorrowthe next day, the following day
yesterdaythe day before, the previous day
here / thisthere / that
No backshift needed. If something is still true when you report it, you can keep the original tense: He said the meeting is on Friday (and it still is).
Common mistakes. Do not keep question order: She asked where did I live is wrong; say She asked where I lived. And tell needs a person: He told me he was leaving, but He said he was leaving.

Practice

  1. "I'm working from home today," Sara said.
  2. "Have you seen my keys?" he asked me.
  3. "Where is the station?" the tourist asked.
  4. "Don't touch the screen," the teacher told us.
  5. "We will finish the report tomorrow," they said.
  6. Choose: He (said / told) me that he was leaving.
Show answers
  1. Sara said she was working from home that day.
  2. He asked me if I had seen his keys.
  3. The tourist asked where the station was.
  4. The teacher told us not to touch the screen.
  5. They said they would finish the report the next day.
  6. told
