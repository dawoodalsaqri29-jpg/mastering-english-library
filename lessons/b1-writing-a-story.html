Writing a short story

Tell a story about a past event with a clear order, past tenses and time words that link the events.

Download PDF

Stories are a common task in B1 exams and a great way to practise past tenses. A good short story has a clear beginning, a problem, and an ending, joined by time words.

PartWhat to includeUseful language
BeginningWho, where, whenLast summer, I was staying with my cousin in Salalah.
ProblemSomething unexpected happensSuddenly, the car stopped in the middle of the road.
EventsWhat people did, in orderFirst we called for help. After that, we waited for an hour.
EndingHow it finished and how people feltIn the end, a farmer helped us. We were so relieved.

Tenses

TenseUseExample
Past simpleThe main events, one after anotherWe left at six and arrived at noon.
Past continuousBackground, or an action in progress when something happenedIt was raining when the bus broke down.
Time words make a story easy to follow. First, then, after that, suddenly, while, as soon as, in the end. Add one feeling adjective at the end: relieved, proud, embarrassed.
Common mistake. Switching between present and past: We went to the beach and suddenly my brother falls in the water. Keep the whole story in the past: fell.

Practice

  1. Put the verb in the past simple: We ___ (go) to the market early.
  2. Choose the right tense: I (walked / was walking) home when I saw the accident.
  3. Correct the mistake: Yesterday I miss the bus, so I take a taxi.
  4. Add a time word: ___ we finished dinner, we went for a walk.
  5. Order the parts: (a) In the end, we found the keys in my bag. (b) Last Friday, we were going to the airport. (c) Suddenly, I realised I didn't have the keys.
Show answers
  1. went
  2. was walking
  3. Yesterday I missed the bus, so I took a taxi.
  4. After / As soon as
  5. b, c, a
