Now, let’s look at eight wonderful words from the story. Listen, and say each word with me.

Brittle. Something brittle is hard and dry, and it breaks very easily. It was a folded sheet of parchment, yellow and brittle.

Glimmer. To glimmer means to shine with a faint, gentle light. A thin line of light began to glimmer across the faded ink.

Venture. To venture means to go somewhere new, or a little bit dangerous. They had never ventured so far from home.

Reluctant. If you are reluctant, you do not really want to do something. Mia was reluctant to leave such a magical place.

Astonished. Astonished means extremely surprised. Mia was so astonished that she could not speak at all.

Cherish. To cherish something is to love it and take great care of it. Mia keeps the old map, and she will cherish it all her life.

Treacherous. A treacherous place is full of hidden dangers. At the slippery stream, Mia said, “This looks treacherous.”

Resilient. Resilient people recover quickly after something hard happens. Mia said, “We’re resilient, you and I. We fall down, and we get back up.”
