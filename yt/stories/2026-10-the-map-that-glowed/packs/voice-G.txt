“Plant these seeds where you will see them every day, and cherish the people who wait for you at home. With love, Nell.”

“Nell,” Mia whispered. “Grandma Nell!” Long ago, her grandmother had been a girl just like her.

Mia was reluctant to leave such a magical place, but she knew someone was waiting.

She took the seeds and the letter, left her yellow ribbon in the box for the next child, and gently closed the lid.

On the map, a new line of light had begun to glow. It led straight home.

Far below, at the cottage gate, a small lantern was shining. Grandma Nell was waiting.

“Did you find it?” Grandma asked, her eyes twinkling. Mia ran into her arms. “I found it, Grandma. And I found you.”

Grandma saw the scraped knee and smiled. “Brave and resilient,” she said. “That is how every true explorer comes home.”

Then she stroked Whiskers’ silky head. “Shadow had emerald eyes, too,” she said softly. “Some magic runs in families.”

That spring, Mia and Grandma planted the silver seeds beneath the kitchen window.

Now, whenever the moon rises, the flowers glow, filling the cottage with soft silver light.

Mia keeps the brittle old map in a box beside her bed. She will cherish it all her life.

Because on that hill, she learned something. The greatest treasures are not buried in the ground.

They are the people we love, the friends who walk beside us, and the courage we find when we need it most.

And who knows? In some dusty attic near you, another map may be waiting to glow. The end.
