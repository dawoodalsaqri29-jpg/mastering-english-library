Question one. What made the map begin to glow? A, the setting sun. B, Whiskers’ paw. Or C, a magic word.

The answer is B. The light began to glimmer when Whiskers’ shimmering paw touched the map.

Question two. How did Mia find her way through the mist? A, she followed the stars. B, she carried a lantern. Or C, she listened for Whiskers’ bell.

The answer is C. She tied a ribbon to his collar and followed the sound of his little bell.

Question three. Who wrote the letter in the tin box? A, Grandma Nell. B, a famous explorer. Or C, Mia’s teacher.

The answer is A. Grandma Nell wrote it when she was nine years old. Well done!
