Chapter two. Into the Whispering Hills.

Mia and Whiskers set off along the dirt lane, past the last cottage and the old stone well.

Swallows dipped over the meadows, and the long grass whispered as the warm breeze combed through it.

“We’re venturing into the hills,” Mia said, “just like real explorers!”

Whiskers trotted ahead, his tail held high like a little black flag.

At last they reached the crooked oak. Its twisted branches looked like an old man stretching after a long sleep.

Mia sat among its roots and spread the map across her knees.

Suddenly, a gust of wind came rushing over the hilltop.

It snatched the brittle map right out of her hands and sent it tumbling through the air.

“No!” Mia cried. The map was fluttering straight towards a thicket of thorny brambles.

But Whiskers was already running. He leapt, twisting in mid-air, his fur flashing silver in the sun.

With the softest touch, he trapped the map between his front paws and landed just before the thorns.

He had not put a single claw through it, and he looked very pleased with himself.

Mia scooped him up and laughed with relief. “You clever, clever cat!”

After that, she kept the map safely in her satchel.

Now the glowing line pointed down into a shadowy valley, loud with the roar of water.
