Now, let’s look at eight beautiful words from the story. Listen carefully, and say each one with me.

Intricate. Something intricate is made of many small parts that fit together in a detailed way. Master Tobin’s clocks were intricate little worlds of springs and wheels.

Meticulous. A meticulous person is very careful about every small detail. He was meticulous. He polished every gear twice.

Fleeting. Something fleeting lasts only a very short time. But the pride he felt was fleeting, like breath on a cold window.

Linger. To linger means to stay a little longer, slowly, before going. The smell of oil and wood still lingered.

Irreversible. If something is irreversible, you cannot change it back. Some things are irreversible, Elias.

Solemn. Solemn means very serious and quiet. The little owl looked at Elias with a very solemn face.

Redeem. To redeem yourself is to make up for something wrong you have done. You cannot redeem a mistake by hiding it. You redeem it by facing it.

Conscience. Your conscience is the voice inside you that tells you what is right and wrong. It was his conscience, and it did not sound pleased.
