Question one. What happens when Elias turns the silver hourglass? A: the whole town goes back one hour. B: the snow stops forever. Or C: Master Tobin becomes young again.

The answer is A. The whole town goes back one hour, but only once each night.

Question two. Why does Elias not turn back time after the globe breaks? A: Pip takes the key away. B: he chooses to face his mistake honestly. Or C: the hourglass is broken.

The answer is B. He chooses to face his mistake, because hiding it would never redeem it.

Question three. Which word means impossible to change back? A: fleeting. B: irreversible. Or C: meticulous.

The answer is B: irreversible. A broken promise is irreversible, but it can always be mended.
