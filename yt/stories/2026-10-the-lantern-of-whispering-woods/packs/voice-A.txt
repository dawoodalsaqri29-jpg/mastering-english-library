Chapter one. The Fading Light.

At the edge of the Whispering Woods, in a small stone cottage with a mossy roof, there lived a girl named Noor.

Every night, Noor fell asleep to the glow of the forest outside her window.

Mushrooms shone like tiny blue moons between the roots. Fireflies drifted through the branches like sparks from a gentle fire.

And the trees themselves would murmur soft songs that no one could quite understand, but everyone could feel.

But this autumn, something was wrong.

Night by night, the glow began to dwindle, the way a candle shrinks when no one remembers to tend it.

The mushrooms faded to grey. The fireflies vanished one by one. And the songs of the trees became a thin, eerie silence.

Noor sat at her window, chin in hands, remembering her grandmother's old story.

“Deep in the heart of the woods,” Grandmother would whisper, “there is an ancient Lantern. As long as it shines, the forest shines too.”

“Then someone must find it,” Noor said. “Before the light is gone for ever.”
