Presume.

It means to believe something is true because it's likely, even without proof.

For example: I presume you've already met my brother.

Useful collocations: I presume that. Presumed innocent. Presume to know.

Speculate.

It means to form ideas about something when you don't have all the facts.

For example: Experts speculate that prices will rise next year.

Useful collocations: Speculate about. Widely speculated. It's too early to speculate.

Surmise.

It's a formal word. It means to guess something from the small clues you have.

For example: From her silence, I surmised that she was upset.

Useful collocations: Surmise that. One can only surmise. As you may have surmised.

Envisage.

It means to imagine something as a future possibility, to picture a plan in your mind.

For example: We envisage opening a second branch in twenty twenty-seven.

Useful collocations: Envisage a future. Envisage that. It is envisaged that.
