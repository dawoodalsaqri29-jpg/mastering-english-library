Let's review. Thinking carefully: ponder and contemplate.

Thinking with others, or again and again: deliberate and ruminate.

Guessing: presume, speculate, and surmise.

Picturing the future: envisage.

Memory trick: a cow is a ruminant. It chews the same food again and again. When you ruminate, you chew the same thought again and again.
