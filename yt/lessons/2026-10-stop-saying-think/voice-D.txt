Now, pronunciation. Many of these verbs end in -ate. Put the stress three syllables from the end: CON-tem-plate, SPEC-u-late, RU-mi-nate.

Careful with deliberate. The verb ends with "rate". The adjective ends with "rut": a de-LIB-er-ut choice.

Two words are stressed at the end: pre-SUME and sur-MISE. And envisage is stressed in the middle: en-VIS-age.

Here's a short story with all eight words. Listen and follow.

Layla had been contemplating a new job for months. Every night, she would ponder the same question: should she stay or leave?

Her friends speculated that she was unhappy, and her manager presumed she would never go. From her quiet mood, her sister surmised the truth.

Finally, Layla stopped ruminating, deliberated with her family, and made her choice. Today, she can't envisage working anywhere else.

Quick question: who guessed the truth from Layla's quiet mood? Pause the video and answer.

Her sister. She surmised it.
