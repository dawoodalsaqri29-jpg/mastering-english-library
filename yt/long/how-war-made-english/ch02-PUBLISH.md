## Description
In 793, monks in the north of England wrote that fiery dragons were seen in the sky. Then the ships came.
This is Part 2 of How War Made English: 250 years of Viking raids, invasion and settlement, the king who hid in
a swamp, and the everyday English words the Northmen left behind: sky, egg, knife, window, law, and even they.

Learn the history, and the words, together. English and Arabic subtitles are available.
