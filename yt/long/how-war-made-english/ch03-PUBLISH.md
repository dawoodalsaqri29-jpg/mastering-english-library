## Description
In 1066, England had three different kings in a single year. One of them marched his army north, won a famous victory,
and nineteen days later had to fight again, on a hill near Hastings.

This is Part 3 of How War Made English: the death of Edward the Confessor, Harald Hardrada's invasion, Stamford Bridge,
the Norman landing, the Battle of Hastings and a coronation on Christmas Day. Then the words: why English has cow and beef,
begin and commence, freedom and liberty, and why even the word "war" came to English with the Normans.

Learn the history, and the words, together. English and Arabic subtitles are available.
