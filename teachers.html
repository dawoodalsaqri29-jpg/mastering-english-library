What is in the free lessons

  • 72 lessons: 4 levels (B1, B2, C1, C2), 6 skills (grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, speaking), 3 per skill at each level.
  • Free to copy for your own classes. Print the lessons and their PDFs for your students; the footer of every PDF says so.
  • Every lesson has practice questions and answers. In the PDF the answers sit on the last page, so you can print the worksheet pages without them.
  • 12 listening lessons: the transcript is on the teacher page; read it aloud, or play it from the lesson's web page (it uses the browser's own English voice; there are no audio files).
  • 59 of the 72 lessons have a "Common mistake" box: an error learners often make on that point, and the fix.

    Privacy

    No accounts, no sign-up, no cookies and no analytics. The "mark as done" list is saved only in the learner's own browser. If you opened this page from a link we sent you (one ending in ?ref=), your browser keeps that short tag for 30 days, only so that a message you send us from this page, or a visit to our store from it, shows which link you came from. Nothing else is recorded. (GitHub, which hosts the site, keeps its own standard server logs.)
